A weighted score cannot override these gates.

All five require operational proof. Keep every candidate’s status “Not validated” until the sandbox evidence is reviewed.

Access isolation

Named partner/distributor users cannot see unauthorized records, fields, files, exports or AI answers. Individual offboarding must revoke sessions and delegated access without disabling the whole distributor.

Workflow parity

Cribl’s provisional approval, final approval, automated denial, extensions and expiration work end to end, including existing/new opportunity linkage, notifications, territory routing and transition history.

Salesforce integrity

Define authoritative objects/fields and ownership. Failed, concurrent or replayed transactions must not create duplicates or silently overwrite authoritative data. Demonstrate retries and monitoring.

Migration continuity

Preserve Salesforce IDs where applicable, or durable legacy-ID mappings elsewhere. Reconcile statuses, timestamps, relationships, attachments and historical reports. Prove rollback and a safe coexistence plan before decommissioning legacy dependencies.

AI governance

Ground answers in permitted sources with citations and human escalation. Write-capable agents require attributable action logs and appropriate approval/confirmation. Start read-only if these controls are insufficient.

Turn the shortlist into a defensible decision.

  1. Confirm scope: incumbent PRM (the local workflow assumes Channelscaler), EULER’s intended product, active external-user count, budget and target cutover.
  2. Request a requirement-level response: every vendor must label each item Standard, Configuration, Custom development, Third-party dependency, Roadmap, or Unsupported.
  3. Run the same sandbox tests: use representative sanitized Cribl data and named partner/distributor users. Record outcomes, configuration effort and unresolved gaps.
  4. Prove migration before contracting: reconcile IDs/history and reporting, test rollback, and identify every legacy managed-package or integration dependency to retire.
  5. Select on demonstrated fit and TCO: use the evidence scores to prioritize questions, not to bypass a failed operational gate.
